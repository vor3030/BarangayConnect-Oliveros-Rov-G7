How do I review and approve a document request?+

Open Request queue, select Review on a Pending application, verify the resident's purpose and copy count, choose Approved or Rejected, and save your remarks.

When are staff remarks mandatory?+

Clear explanation remarks are always required when marking a request as Rejected so the resident knows what to correct before submitting again.

Can a Completed or Rejected request be reopened?+

No. Once marked Completed or Rejected, requests stay closed to preserve audit history. Staff may remove rejected test or duplicate records from the review screen if needed.

How do I update my staff contact details?+

Open Edit profile to update your name, email address, contact number, or profile photo.

{% else %}
How do I request a barangay document?+

Open Document requests, choose your certificate type, state your purpose and number of copies, and submit. Your request starts as Pending and updates live as staff review it.

Can I edit or cancel a submitted request?+

Yes, while a request is still Pending you can edit its details or cancel it from the request tracking page. Once Approved, Completed, or Rejected, the request is locked.

How do I update my details?+

Open Edit profile to update your name, email, or contact number. Select Save changes when you're done.

Can I sign in with my username?+

Yes. Use your email address or username with the same password. Capitalization doesn't matter.

How do I add a profile photo?+

Choose Profile photo, upload a JPG, PNG, or WebP image up to 5 MB, and save your changes.
