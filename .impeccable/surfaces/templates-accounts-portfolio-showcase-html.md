# Barangay Connect — Surface Brief & Direction Contract

- Scope: Landing page (`/`), Authentication (`/login/`, `/register/`), Resident & Admin Dashboards, Profile, and Online Document Request workflows.
- Visitor modes: Persuade / Gateway on `/`; Operate across all authenticated portal views.

## Direction contract
- THESIS: Fuse atmospheric twilight Philippine highland photography with crisp smoked-glass and pure-white floating action surfaces so Barangay Connect feels like a calm, high-trust modern civic sanctuary rather than a generic cream-and-serif government template or developer UI showcase.
- OWN-WORLD: Deep twilight slate-pine (`#0D1518`, `#101B1F`), misty fjord blue-slate (`#4D6F7D`), translucent hairline glass frame (`rgba(235, 244, 246, 0.24)`), dark smoked-glass floating panels (`rgba(11, 20, 24, 0.78)`), pure-white floating pill command bars (`#FFFFFF`), warm champagne-sand primary CTAs (`#E7D4B5`), hearth-amber lantern indicators (`#E59A3C`), crisp mist-stone operating canvas (`#F2F5F4`), and single-family `Manrope Variable` geometric typography with light/bold weight contrast.
- STORY: Visitors immediately understand they can request barangay documents online, pick a document directly in the floating launcher bar or smoked-glass service card, track processing in real time, and sign in to a calm role-specific workspace with zero "Interface showcase" developer chrome.
- FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed framed twilight sanctuary hero (`100svh` max, rounded architectural glass rim) with transparent top navigation bar (`Services`, `Workflow`, `Community`, `Sign in`, crisp white `Open portal` pill), stacked left-aligned dual-opacity geometric display headline, floating bottom-right smoked-glass interactive document estimator card with white pill CTA, floating pure-white pill service launcher bar with champagne CTA, and bottom-right white floating Help Desk support pill.
